01 Origin

Outside of work, my number one hobby is engineering — so I come home from work to work. Besides that I'm into reading, 3D printing, sports, coding, machine learning and finance. Curiosity is the engine; building things carefully is the habit.

From CAD to chips on the floor

The spindle you're watching is my own build, running a real job. Steel for stiffness, printed brackets for alignment, the rest dialed in by hand until it cut clean.

Python · toolpaths

A slicer & G-code generator, from scratch

I architected a custom STL-to-G-code pipeline from the ground up — the foundation for a bigger research idea: reducing support material with non-standard slicing angles. Rebuilding slicing mechanics in Python gave me total control over toolpath generation, which off-the-shelf slicers simply won't hand you.

Iterate · prototype

3D printing as a way of thinking

Printing started as a hobby and became how I engineer: iterate fast, prototype early, learn by building. As a broke college student every print felt like "printing with diamonds" — so each iteration had to earn its filament.
