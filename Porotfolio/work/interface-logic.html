Interface Logic & Access Control

Right action obvious, wrong action hard.

Re-architected the vehicle's menu system around safety classes and operator access levels. The interface itself decides which controls are even reachable, given who is driving and what the vehicle is doing.

Overview

Industrial vehicle interfaces accumulate options over years. By the time a fleet manager, a technician, and an operator all need different things, the menu becomes a hazard surface in itself. The redesign brief: stop showing operators controls they shouldn't touch, and stop burying the controls technicians need.
