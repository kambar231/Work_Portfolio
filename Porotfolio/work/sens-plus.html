Smart Environment Sensor Plus

Stereoscopic perception, tied to the brake actuator.

Stereoscopic camera detection for pedestrians and obstacles, integrated directly with regenerative braking. ML-based perception wired into the vehicle's safety stack — not a notification, an action.

Overview

Pedestrian-aware perception is well understood in passenger cars. Less so in industrial machines, where false positives cost throughput and false negatives cost lives. SEnS+ had to deliver real-time detection that can tell a person from a stack of pallets, then turn that detection into measurable braking force.
