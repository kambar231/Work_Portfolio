Steer Differentiation System

Sharper geometry, same operator feel.

Real-time refinement of steering geometry using high-fidelity sensor resolution — tighter handling envelopes without compromising the operator's feel for the machine.

Overview

Steering systems on industrial vehicles trade resolution for robustness; the sensors are coarse and the controllers compensate with smoothing. That works, until you want to fuse steering data with stability or perception systems that need cleaner inputs. The goal here was to lift the resolution floor.
