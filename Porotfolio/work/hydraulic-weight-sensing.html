Hydraulic Weight Sensing

The vehicle weighs the load itself.

Real-time load weighing using pressure transducers built into the hydraulic manifold. Drives operator display feedback and automatic speed reduction once the load crosses the rated envelope.

Overview

Overloaded vehicles are a leading cause of preventable damage — to product, machine, and operator. Conventional weighing needs a known stop, a separate scale, or external instrumentation. The aim was to make weighing happen continuously and invisibly, using the hydraulics that were already on the vehicle.
