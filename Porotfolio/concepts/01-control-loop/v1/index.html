DEPLOY · VEH-001

Pedestrian Detection, Automated Braking & Stability

The Raymond Corporation · 2024 – present
Problem
Industrial vehicles handling shifting loads sit between two failures: an operator who can't feel danger coming, and a safety system so cautious it steals the performance they need.
My role
Design, implement and validate the electromechanical and control systems — sensor fusion, the detection pipeline, and the braking/stability response.
The loop
Cameras & sensors sense people and obstacles → the controller decides risk by distance and speed → the truck responds with graded warnings and automated braking.
Impact
Measurable gains in performance, energy efficiency and operational safety — a person who'd otherwise be in a blind spot gets seen.
Sensor fusionMachine visionEmbedded CCANRegenerative braking

SIMULATE · ACA-003

Flight Dynamics Simulation

RIT · MATLAB & Simulink
Problem
Model how nine different aircraft respond to varied flight conditions — accurately enough to trust the response, not just the trend.
My role
Built the simulation in MATLAB + Simulink using Euler-angle and quaternion attitude models — quaternion over Euler to dodge gimbal lock.
The loop
State estimate senses attitude → control model decides corrections → dynamics respond; the traces settle onto the commanded state.
Impact
A reusable framework for response analysis across the full aircraft set.

RIT · Ansys
Problem
Verify the Rocketdyne F-1 engine nozzle (Saturn V / Apollo 11; 7.5M lb thrust) survives operating stresses without failure.

The loop
Define loads → simulate the structural response → decide whether the design holds. Simulation before steel.
Impact
Confirmed no failure under operating stresses for one of history's most extreme engines.

Double Pendulum

RIT · model vs. rig
Problem
Predict chaotic double-pendulum behavior under set initial conditions — then see if the math survives contact with a real build.
My role
Built the theoretical model and validated it against a physical rig — chaos is where small modeling errors become visible fast.
The loop
A clean lesson in sensitivity: sense the initial state, the dynamics decide everything after. Trust the sim — and know when not to.
Impact
Model and rig agreed within the sensitivity you'd expect from a chaotic system.
