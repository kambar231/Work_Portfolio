CONCEPT 08 — THE TOOLBOX  ·  v1
Created 2026-06-17

A new revision (does NOT replace 07 The Workbench). Adds the Stephen King
"On Writing" toolbox metaphor: you build a multi-tier toolbox over a lifetime,
keep your most-used tools on top, and carry it so you're never empty-handed when
the work appears. Here the site IS an engineer's tool chest — five drawers, top =
what Kambar reaches for daily, bottom = the foundational tools earned slowly.
Each drawer pulls open to reveal its TOOLS (skills) and the PROJECTS that came
out of them (real photos). Live double-pendulum + step-response carry the
control-loop soul from concept 01.

Built against a deep-research anti-AI rubric (avoid: default fonts, neon gradients,
glassmorphism, blobs, even card grids, "scroll to explore"; emulate: editorial
type, asymmetry, restraint, real photography, one accent, weighted motion, an
authored point of view). Type: Archivo (industrial display) + Fraunces (voice) +
IBM Plex Mono (tool labels). Palette: Kambar's own ink/ember/steel/brass.

Self-contained index.html + ./assets (real, warm-graded). King text is paraphrased
(copyright-safe). Immutable snapshot — iterate by copying to ../v2/.
